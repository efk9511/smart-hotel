About Seaside Hotel

Nestled along the pristine coastline, Seaside Hotel blends modern luxury with the tranquil beauty of the ocean. Our technology-driven platform ensures a seamless and memorable stay for every guest.

Our intelligent system manages room reservations, common area bookings, maintenance requests, and announcements — all from a single, user-friendly interface. Whether you are here for business or leisure, we deliver an experience that is as smart as it is serene.

Our Mission

To provide a smart, efficient, and unforgettable seaside escape for every guest while empowering hotel staff and management with powerful tools to deliver exceptional service.

Features

  • Easy online room booking with sea-view options
  • Common area reservations (Infinity Pool, Spa, Rooftop Lounge & more)
  • 24/7 maintenance and concierge ticket system
  • Real-time availability and pricing
  • Role-based dashboards for guests, staff, and managers
  • Dark and light theme support
  • Smart reporting and analytics
